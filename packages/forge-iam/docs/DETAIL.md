# Enterprise IAM Sign-in

Sign in with your enterprise account, manage your AI gateway key, and enable enterprise models.

## Features

- **Enterprise Sign-in** (`Settings → Enterprise account`): Sign in and manage your enterprise session.
- **AI Gateway Key**: Configure your personal enterprise gateway key separately from public personal keys.
- **Enterprise Models** (`Settings → Enterprise models`): Enable models from the enterprise catalog, add custom models, and adjust model limits.

## Usage

1. Go to **Settings → Enterprise account** to sign in and enter your AI gateway key.
2. Go to **Settings → Enterprise models** to enable models, then select them in chat.
