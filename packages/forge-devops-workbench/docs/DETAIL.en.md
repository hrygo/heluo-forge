# DOP Workbench

Connect to the enterprise DOP delivery workbench in DeepSeek Harness to view cross-platform to-dos, track progress across requirements, releases, and projects, and collaborate through the full R&D lifecycle right in chat.

## Key Capabilities

- **To-Do Aggregation & Progress Boards**: Aggregates 7 to-do channels together with Requirement, Implementation, and Project progress boards, with direct links back to DOP pages.
- **Full-Lifecycle R&D Delivery**: Covers requirements, projects, development, testing, and efficiency domains. Phase 1 highlights one-sentence requirement drafting, RMP outline & section inspection, and guided user-story splitting into the unsent chat draft box.
- **Unified Enterprise IAM & Quick Intranet Switching**: Authenticates automatically via Enterprise Account (IAM) with zero separate tokens. Switch to your intranet DOP endpoint via private-bundle defaults, `HELUO_DOP_BASE_URL` (or `FORGE_DEVOPS_DOP_ENDPOINT`), or the Connection Settings panel below.

## Usage

1. Click **DOP Workbench** in the top-left sidebar to open the right-hand tab.
2. Set `HELUO_DOP_BASE_URL` or enter your DOP base URL under Connection Settings and click Save.
3. Ensure you are signed in under Settings → Enterprise Account (IAM).
